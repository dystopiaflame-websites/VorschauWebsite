Our Story

Created for people who love good coffee and beautiful everyday moments.

Maison Café was born from a simple idea: a café should feel like a small escape. A place where the coffee is excellent, the food is handmade and the atmosphere makes people want to stay a little longer.

From the first espresso in the morning to a relaxed afternoon with friends, we focus on quality, hospitality and a warm neighborhood feeling.
